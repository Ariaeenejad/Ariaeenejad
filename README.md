## About Me

I am a faculty member at the **Agricultural Biotechnology Research Institute of Iran (ABRII)**, working in the field of **Systems and Synthetic Biology** with a focus on enzyme biotechnology, metagenomics, computational biology, and sustainable bioprocess development.

My research integrates **metagenomic exploration, computational approaches, molecular and biochemical characterization, and enzyme engineering** to discover and develop novel biocatalysts with improved functional properties.

A major focus of my work is the discovery and characterization of **metagenome-derived enzymes**, including laccases, xylanases, β-glucosidases, phytases, and other industrially relevant enzymes.

I am particularly interested in translating enzyme discovery into practical applications in **agriculture, environmental biotechnology, lignocellulosic biomass conversion, bioremediation, animal nutrition, and sustainable industrial processes**.

My research also explores enzyme stabilization and immobilization using advanced biomaterials and nanostructured carriers to improve the activity, stability, reusability, and industrial applicability of biocatalysts.
